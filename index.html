5 Minutes to Jam

Goal: get non-musicians to play around on a piano in 5 minutes

Try it: click the keys, or type A W S E D F T G Y H U J K

Musical Notes

Western music uses 12 notes, repeated across the keyboard
White keys: C D E F G A B, also called Do Ré Mi Fa Sol La Si.
Black keys: sharp ♯ or flat ♭ notes.

Semitones

Neighbouring keys are one semitone apart.

Major and Minor chords

Major: root + 4 + 3
Minor: root + 3 + 4

Major and Minor Scales

A scale is a consecutive series of notes that form a progression between one note and its octave.
